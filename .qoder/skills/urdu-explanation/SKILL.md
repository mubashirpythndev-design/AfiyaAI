# Urdu Explanation Skill

Convert grounded information into:
- Simple Urdu
- Roman Urdu
- English (when requested)

## Rules
- Preserve medicine names accurately.
- Preserve dosage/strength exactly.
- Do NOT translate medicine names incorrectly.
- Do NOT add medical claims not present in the retrieved context.
- Keep language simple.
- Preserve expressions of uncertainty.
